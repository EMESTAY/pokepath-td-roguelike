# Roguelike Mode Documentation

## Overview

Roguelike Mode is a new game mode that focuses on adaptability and strategic resource management. Unlike the Standard mode which uses a Draft system, Roguelike mode forces you to build your team dynamically wave by wave.

## Core Rules

1.  **Random Start**: You begin with **one random Starter Pokémon** (Tier 1). No initial draft.
2.  **Reward Phase**: After completing each wave, the game pauses and offers you **3 Random Rewards**. You must choose one.
3.  **Mandatory Choice**: You **MUST** choose a reward to proceed. The reward window cannot be closed or skipped.
4.  **One Choice Per Wave**: You cannot take multiple rewards. Choose wisely between immediate power (Items/Levels) or long-term growth (New Pokemon/Gold).
5.  **Permadeath**: (Standard Rule) If your lives reach 0, the run ends.

## Mechanics

### 💰 Scaling Interest

- **The Bank**: Every time you finish a wave, you gain **+10% Interest** on your **currently held Gold**.
- _Strategy_: Saving gold early generates massive wealth in late waves, but makes surviving early waves harder.

### 🔄 Reroll System

- Don't like your 3 choices? Press **REROLL**.
- **Cost**: Starts at **500g**. Increases by 50% for each subsequent reroll in the same wave (500 -> 750 -> 1125...).
- Cost resets to 500g at the start of a new wave.

### ⚔️ Duplicate Handling (Upgrades)

- If you select a Reward Card for a Pokémon you **already have**:
  - It does **NOT** add a second copy to your team.
  - Instead, it **UPGRADES** your existing Pokémon (grants massive XP/Levels).
  - _Strategy_: Use this to build "Tall" (a few very high-level carries) instead of "Wide".

### 🧠 Smart Loot (Synergy)

- The game analyzes your team's **Types**.
- Rewards have a slightly higher chance to match your team's elemental types (e.g., if you have Fire types, you might see more Fire items/Pokemon).
- Look for the **"SYNERGY"** tag on cards!

### 🌊 Zone Synergies

- **Route 1 (Water Pond)**:
  - If you occupy **ALL 5 Water Tiles** in the pond with **Water-type Pokémon** (or Pokemon that can swim), they will all receive a **Synergy Bonus**.
  - **Bonus**: +10% Power and +10% Attack Speed.
  - _Visual_: A pulsing cyan aura will appear around them.

### 📦 Team Management

- **Team Limit**: You have a limited number of slots in your active team.
- **Overcrowding**: If you acquire a new Pokémon reward when your team is full, it will be automatically sent to the **PC Box**.
- You can manage your team between waves in the Team Manager.

## Rewards & Items

### 🐾 Pokémon Cards

- Adds a new Pokémon to your team.
- Displays Ability and Type.
- **Rarity**: Common (Gray), Rare (Blue), Legendary (Gold).

### 🎒 Held Items

- Standard items like **Protein**, **Magnet**, **Leftovers**.
- Automatically added to your Inventory.

### 🧪 Consumables (New!)

- **Rare Candy**: Instantly grants limits XP / Levels to a random or chosen unit.
- **Potion**: Restores **1 Life** (Heart) to the Player. Very rare!

### 🟡 Gold Piles

- Instant injection of cash.
- "Critical" Gold piles (Gold Border) give double stats.
